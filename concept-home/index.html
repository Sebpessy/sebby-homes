The Concept

Drawn before it is built.

Concept Residence No. 01 is a design study: a two-story contemporary home for a Dallas–Fort Worth lot, about 4,400 sq ft under air. It is not a client's home. It shows how we plan every Sebby home, with the whole house resolved in 3D, room by room and layer by layer, before the slab is poured.

  • 4 bedrooms, 4.5 baths, a glass wine room under the stair, and a 3-car garage

Questions

About this drawing.

Is the concept home a real house?

No. Concept Residence No. 01 is a design study by Sebby Homes, drawn to show how we plan a custom home. It is not a client's home and not a house for sale.

Can I see my own home in 3D before it is built?

Yes. On a Sebby Homes custom home, the plans are modeled in 3D during design, so you can see the rooms, the volumes, and how the house goes together before the slab is poured.

How big is the concept home?

About 4,400 square feet on two stories: 2,720 square feet on level 1 with the primary suite, kitchen, two-story family room and study, and 1,660 square feet on level 2 with three bedrooms and a game room, plus a three-car garage, a covered patio and a pool.
