Questions

Before you plan one.

Can a closet, nook, or space under the stairs become a wine room?

Often, yes. The wine room on this page started as an empty window nook. What matters is where the space sits in the house, whether a cooling unit can vent, and how the glass and racking fit. We look at that on a walk of the house before anything is drawn.

Does a wine room need its own cooling?

A sealed room meant to store wine long term needs dedicated cooling, insulation, and a vapor barrier, all built in before the racking goes up. A display wall in the open, conditioned part of the house may not. We tell you which one your plan is before it is priced.

Can a wine room be added during a larger renovation?

Yes. Building it alongside a kitchen, bar, or whole-home remodel is often the simplest time, because the walls are already open for the electrical, the condensate line, and the cooling.
