The Invisible Craft.

"In a true custom build, you don't see the systems."

Linear slot diffusers flush to the ceiling. Concealed returns. No visible trim, no clumsy reveals. Audio, video, and climate integrated into the architecture. The engineering disappears. The architecture remains.

08 · Questions

Questions, answered.

What owners ask most about building a custom home with Sebby Homes in Dallas–Fort Worth.

What areas does Sebby Homes build in?

Sebby Homes builds custom luxury homes across Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas, including Highland Park, Preston Hollow, University Park, Southlake, Westlake, Uptown, and Knox–Henderson. As a design-led custom builder rooted in DFW, we know the lots, neighborhoods, and permitting in each of these communities.

Does Sebby Homes build on my lot?

Yes. Sebby Homes builds custom homes on your lot anywhere in the Dallas–Fort Worth area. The process starts with a conversation and a walk of your site, where we read the land together before design begins.

What makes Sebby Homes different from a production or volume builder?

Sebby Homes is a design-led custom builder with single accountability (one builder, one schedule, one point of contact), not a production builder working from fixed floor plans. Architecture, interiors, and budget are resolved together before construction, and the finish is held to a standard of flush reveals, aligned grain, and shadow-gap millwork.

How long does it take to build a custom home in Dallas–Fort Worth?

A custom home from Sebby Homes typically takes about 14 to 24 months from first design to handover, depending on the size of the home, the complexity of the site, and city permitting. The five stages are Design, Preconstruction, Build, Finish-Out, and Handover.

How much does a custom home cost with Sebby Homes?

Custom home pricing with Sebby Homes depends on the lot, the size of the home, and the level of finish, and it is resolved transparently during design and preconstruction rather than quoted from a template. See our detailed breakdown of what it costs to build a custom home in Dallas–Fort Worth.

How does the Sebby Homes process work?

Sebby Homes follows a five-stage process: Design (architecture, interiors, and budget as one), Preconstruction (engineering, permits, and selections), Build (disciplined site leadership with weekly owner updates), Finish-Out (the invisible craft of millwork, systems, and light), and Handover (final walkthrough, systems training, and warranty care). Clients follow every step through the six9 Builder client portal.

How do I start a project with Sebby Homes?

To start a custom home project with Sebby Homes, contact founder Seb Pessy at 469-996-3789 or seb@sebby.homes. The first step is a simple conversation about your site and program, followed by a walk of your lot in the Dallas–Fort Worth area.
