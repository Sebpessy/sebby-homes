How much does it cost to build a custom home in Dallas–Fort Worth?

By Seb Pessy, Founder of Sebby Homes · Updated July 2026

In 2026, a custom home in Dallas–Fort Worth generally costs about $400 to $900+ per square foot to build, not including land. Where a project lands in that range comes down to four things: the lot, the size of the home, the level of finish, and how well the budget is managed from the first day of design.

That range is wide on purpose: a custom home is not a commodity, and honest numbers depend on real decisions. Below is how we at Sebby Homes think about cost with owners building in Highland Park, Preston Hollow, Southlake, Westlake, Uptown, and the wider Dallas–Fort Worth area.

The four things that move the number

1. The lot

Land is budgeted separately from construction, and in premier DFW neighborhoods it can rival or exceed the cost of the house itself. Beyond the purchase price, the lot's slope, soil, trees, drainage, and utility access all affect site work and foundation cost before a single wall goes up.

2. Size and complexity

Square footage sets the baseline, but complexity multiplies it. A larger footprint, more rooflines, taller ceilings, structural spans for open living, basements, and elevators all raise the per-square-foot figure. A 5,000-square-foot home with intricate geometry can cost more per foot than a simpler home twice its size.

3. Level of finish

This is where custom homes separate from production homes. Stone selections, custom millwork, steel windows, integrated lighting, spa bathrooms, and the invisible craft of a tight building envelope and well-designed mechanical systems can move a project hundreds of dollars per square foot. Finish is also where owners most often underestimate. It is worth pricing selections early, during design.

4. How the budget is managed

The most controllable cost driver is process. When architecture, interiors, and budget are resolved together before construction, rather than discovered during it, expensive changes, delays, and rework are avoided. This is the core of how Sebby Homes works: a single builder accountable for one schedule and one transparent plan.

Line items owners underestimate

  • Site work & foundation: grading, drainage, retaining, and engineered foundations on DFW's expansive clay soils.
  • Permitting & municipal requirements: which vary meaningfully between Dallas, Fort Worth, Southlake, Westlake, and the Park Cities.
  • The invisible envelope: insulation, air sealing, water management, and HVAC design that you never see but feel every day.
  • Landscape, pool, and outdoor living: often scoped late and underfunded.
  • Contingency: a healthy reserve for the unknowns every custom project carries.

How we price a Sebby Homes project

Rather than quote a per-foot number from a template, we resolve budget transparently during design and preconstruction, tied to your actual lot and selections. You see the plan and the numbers together, and you follow every decision through our client portal. That way the budget is a shared, living document, not a surprise at the end.
