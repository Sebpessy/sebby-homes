How long does it take to build a custom home in Dallas–Fort Worth?

By Sebastien Pessy, Founder of Sebby Homes · Updated July 2026

A custom home in Dallas–Fort Worth typically takes about 14 to 24 months from the first design meeting to move-in — roughly 4 to 8 months of design and preconstruction, then 10 to 16 months of construction. The exact timeline depends on the size of the home, the complexity of your lot, and city permitting.

"How long will it take?" is one of the first questions every owner asks Sebby Homes, and the honest answer is that a custom home is a schedule you shape, not one you inherit. Here is how the months actually break down.

The five stages, in months

1. Design — 3 to 5 months

Architecture, interiors, and budget are resolved together. This is the stage that most affects everything after it: time invested here in resolving the plan and selections is what keeps the build on schedule later.

2. Preconstruction — 1 to 3 months

Engineering, permits, and final selections. Permitting timelines vary meaningfully across Dallas, Fort Worth, Southlake, Westlake, and the Park Cities, and this is often the least predictable stretch because it depends on the municipality.

3. Build — 8 to 12 months

Foundation, framing, dry-in, mechanical systems, and the exterior. On DFW's expansive clay soils, foundation work is engineered carefully, and weather can add time. You receive weekly owner updates through our client portal throughout.

4. Finish-Out — 2 to 4 months

The invisible-craft stage — millwork, cabinetry, stone, lighting, and the final layer of detail that separates a custom home from a production one. It rewards patience.

5. Handover — a few weeks

Final walkthrough, systems training, and the start of warranty care.

What makes it faster or slower

  • Decision speed. The single biggest variable you control is how quickly selections are finalized. Changes after construction starts are the top cause of delay.
  • Lot complexity. Slope, soil, trees, drainage, and utility access all affect the early schedule.
  • Size and detail. A larger, more intricate home simply has more to build and finish.
  • Municipality. Permit review times differ city to city across DFW.
The fastest custom homes are not rushed — they are decided early.
