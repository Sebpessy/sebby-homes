Sebby Homes Is Now a BBB Accredited Business

By Seb Pessy, Founder of Sebby Homes · October 2026

I'm proud to share that Sebby Homes is now accredited by the Better Business Bureau in Dallas.

Accreditation means we've committed to the BBB Standards for Trust: advertise honestly, tell the truth, be transparent, honor our promises, respond to every concern, protect your privacy and act with integrity. Those are the same standards I've built this company on since my years in remodeling.

Building or renovating a home is one of the biggest decisions a family makes, and you should be able to check who you're hiring. The seal on this site links straight to our BBB profile.

Thank you to the BBB team in Dallas for welcoming us, and to every client who has trusted us with their home.
