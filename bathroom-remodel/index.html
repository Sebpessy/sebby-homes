Design

Layout, tile, glass, vanities, and lighting are drawn together, with drain locations and the shower layout settled early.

02

Plumbing, drains, venting, and the shower waterproofing go in while the walls are open, before any tile is set.

03

Finish

Tile, glass, stone, and fixtures go in last, aligned to the room, with the same eye for detail as our custom homes.

Questions

Before you plan one.

Can a tub become a walk-in shower?

Often, yes. What matters is where the drain can go, how the floor is framed, and how the glass fits. We look at that on a walk of the house before anything is drawn.

What happens behind the tile?

The shower is waterproofed and the drains and venting are set before any tile goes on. It is the part you won't see once the room is finished, and the part that decides how it holds up.

Can a bath remodel be part of a larger renovation?

Yes. Baths are often remodeled with the primary suite or a whole-home renovation, and doing it at the same time means the walls are opened once.
