## Beyond the IRC
- **Geotechnical report** — the only real answer for soil bearing and
  active-zone depth on expansive clay. Typical residential geotech with
  borings is a modest line item vs. a re-repair; recommend it on any
  recurring-movement job.
- **Engineer of record** — drilled piers, bell-bottom piers, helical
  piles, push piers: all engineered products/designs. Helical/push pier
  capacities come from the manufacturer's ICC-ES report AND the
  installer's torque/pressure logs — ask for both on repair bids.
- **TDLR / local licensing** — foundation repair contractors in Texas:
  verify insurance and warranty transferability; the warranty document
  is part of the bid comparison.

## Red flags that end the prescriptive path
- Known or suspected expansive clay (most of DFW) → engineered
- Fill soils, sloped sites, high water table → geotech
- Pier height tall relative to width (slenderness) → engineer
- Repeated/seasonal movement history → geotech + drainage study first
